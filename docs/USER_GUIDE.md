# Sorted: a short guide

## Getting going

- **Your household** holds everyone's shared lists. An admin invites people from **Members** (they
  sign in with the Google account for that email). Roles: admins manage people and settings, members
  edit, viewers look.
- **Make a list** from the sidebar's **+** or the home page. Start from a template (bills, chores,
  renewals, home maintenance, trip, big purchases, goals) or a blank list. Import a spreadsheet with
  **Import** (CSV).

## Working in a list

- Click a cell to change it. Add items under each group. Tick items to change many at once.
- On a phone, tap an item's name to open it; the ⋯ menu adds subitems, moves, archives or deletes.
- **Views**: add Kanban, Calendar, Timeline, Workload or Chart tabs; each keeps its own filters.
- **Undo** with Ctrl/⌘+Z (or the ↶ button). Deleted items stay in the bin (🗑) for 30 days.
- Open an item for **comments** (type @ to mention someone), **files** (upload up to 10 MB, saved
  in your shared Drive folder) and its **activity**.

## Automations and reminders

- **Automations** (⚡) on a list: pick a recipe ("when status is Done, set Last done to today",
  "remind me 3 days before the due date") or build your own. Clock-based ones run even when nobody
  has the app open.

## Time, work and overviews

- **Time tracking** column: press ▶ to time something, or add time you already spent. **Timesheet**
  (sidebar) totals it by person or item, with CSV export.
- **My work**: everything assigned to you, by due date. **Search**: Ctrl/⌘+K.
- **Google Calendar**: anything with a due date shows as an all-day event on your own "Sorted – your name" calendar (items assigned to you, or that you created when nobody is assigned). It disappears when the item is Done. Choose alert times in Google Calendar.
- **Groups**: every list has a **Group** column. Change it to move an item to another group (or drag the item). When you make a list you choose its groups: the template's own, Tasks, Bills, Study, Project, or your own.
- **New documents**: in a Files cell, **New document** makes a Google Doc named after the item, attaches it and opens it. Everyone in the household who can edit can edit it (it downloads as Word from File → Download).
- **Accepting tasks**: when someone assigns you an item you get a notification with **Accept** / **Reject** (or mark it read). The item shows who has accepted, who has declined and who it is still waiting on.
- **Dashboards**: numbers, progress, charts, overdue items and more from up to five lists.
- **Daily email**: bell → ⚙ → "Email me a daily summary".

Press **?** anywhere for keyboard shortcuts.

## When getting started is the hard part

- **Focus** (sidebar) shows just one thing to do from the items assigned to you, with how soon it's due. Pick up to three for
  **today's plan** and they come first.
- Feels big? **Break it down** into small steps; the first one is all you need to look at.
- Press **5 / 15 / 25 min** to work on it for a set time; you'll hear when it's up.
- **Not now** skips it guilt-free; **Done** ticks it off.
- Tag small jobs **Quick** and tick "Quick wins only" for low-energy moments.
- Automations → **Escalating reminders** adds gentle-to-firm reminders in one click.
